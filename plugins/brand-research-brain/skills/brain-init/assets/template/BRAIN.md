# Shopify learning guide

Learn from interesting websites to understand businesses, choose products, build a store and grow. `brain.json` holds your goal, stage, language and interests. Missing markets and practical constraints remain unspecified. Existing preferences take precedence over starter defaults.

Use the Karpathy LLM Wiki pattern: keep immutable sources in `raw/`, maintain linked synthesis in `wiki/`, and use these guides as the operating context. Open this folder in Obsidian; Claude or Codex can read and maintain the Markdown files. No special Obsidian integration is required.

One website visit is one learning session: save contributing pages separately as raw records, then write one readable case with three concrete practices and one next action for the learner. Consider user insight/selection, product/brand, storefront/conversion, content, acquisition/growth and SEO, expanding only what the evidence supports. Incomplete material can yield fewer practices; never invent missing evidence.

- `wiki/brands/`: business explanation and concise case learning.
- `wiki/categories/`: product/category and user problem learning.
- `wiki/shopify/`: storefront and conversion methods.
- `wiki/growth/`: content, acquisition, SEO and retention methods.
- `wiki/ideas/`: ideas relevant to your own project.
- `wiki/index.md`, `wiki/log.md`: readable navigation and short learning history.
- `raw/sources/`, `raw/assets/`, `raw/build-log/`: sources, attachments and actual practice records.
- `.brb/`: source fingerprints and processing state; keep mechanical details here.

Create or merge reusable notes when useful, link them to the case and evidence, and preserve existing knowledge and corrections. Do not create pages just to fill folders. Later questions should build on these notes and compare cases where relevant.

Distinguish what was observed, what the brand claims, and your interpretation. Cite sources by name and page section. Website presentation does not establish business performance, representative demand or causality. Do not assume the studied site uses Shopify. No default scores, viability decisions, dimension-review tables or consulting roadmaps are needed. Deeper strategy follows explicit user intent.

Never replace raw sources. Read before updating synthesis; preserve unrelated instructions and notes. New sessions use the packaged `learn.py` helper for one short log entry; unchanged evidence is a no-op unless a deliberate synthesis revision is requested. Legacy records remain readable. Local research does not authorize outreach, purchases or publication.

## Background knowledge reconciliation

Every intake or deeper growth pass reads and checks categories, ideas, shopify and growth. Merge/create substantive additions, retain relevant unchanged notes, and skip unsupported areas. Save all four evidence-specific decisions as `knowledge_reviews` only in `.brb/state.json`; no review tables belong in cases, navigation, logs or chat. Keep reading concise while connecting knowledge. Existing ideas can gain untested applications without pretending suggested actions were completed.

For ideas, first derive the current case's own insight and testable hypothesis from its user task, friction or mechanism; then assess old-note fit. Merge only for a shared user problem/mechanism with a substantive new hypothesis/question. Otherwise create a distinct note when warranted, or retain/skip thin evidence. Do not force the case into an old project or invent a product for every brand. Cross-case application does not replace current-case idea synthesis.

## HTML learning documents

After each intake or deeper growth pass, also create or refresh `reports/<brand-id>.html`: concise Chinese case facts, concrete methods, category, growth and current-case ideas, with references at the end. Honor the user's language choice or explicit Markdown-only opt-out. Support continuous reading, share-card mode and browser printing. Markdown remains the knowledge source; digest provenance stays in `.brb/share/` and rendering adds no research event. Read and merge an existing digest before updating it. Unknown channels/results stay unknown rather than being invented to fill the layout.

## Concrete, readable learning

Start with who buys what, in which situation, what they could use instead and what the learner can try. Explain category and idea insights through a tangible scene or choice instead of abstract mechanisms alone. Clearly labeled hypothetical examples are allowed; they are not verified demand or a chosen niche. A material product-selection question may justify one bounded follow-up comparison. Keep shared evidence limits once at the end, retaining a nearby qualifier where needed for truth.

A growth update retains the case's main story, methods and current-case ideas while updating growth learning. Sparse channel evidence does not prevent a labeled test idea grounded in actual product expression. Account counts and access limits must not become the whole case's subject.

## Explain the case category before application

Category learning first names the studied brand's category and actual products, then explains its distinctive product role/buying reason with evidence, and only then offers a transferable takeaway. Do not substitute the learner's proposed category or lead with implementation burdens. A category move is not proof of first invention or sales causality. Keep the learner's candidate products, technologies and tests in a clearly marked application or ideas section. Apply this to cases, category notes and HTML without requiring extra pages.

## Full growth research in HTML

When a detailed growth note exists, retain a short overview and show its evidenced phases, concrete media examples, social/creator/SEO and retail paths, metrics and learner takeaways in visible HTML chapters. Card mode gives each topic a separate panel. Do not discard the study to fit three bullets or require Markdown to read the full learning. Ordinary intake stays concise and stages are never invented. A presentation-only refresh reuses existing research without API calls, credential access or a new research event; preserve case facts, category and ideas.
