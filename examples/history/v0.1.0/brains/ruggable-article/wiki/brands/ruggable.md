## User job and product mechanism
**Inference:** a plausible job is to keep a furnished home attractive while reducing the inconvenience and replacement anxiety caused by dirty rugs. The reported removable layer changes the cleaning process, which is a more specific mechanism than merely promising easy care. The article discusses households with pets or children, but this is its audience characterization, not a representative interview sample. [[raw/sources/ruggable/s-0ca02a375afad68e0a01]] — Original Content / WHO 用户; WHAT 产品.

**Hypothesis:** functional maintenance relief may support an aesthetic product rather than forcing buyers to choose between appearance and care. Reusable pads and replaceable covers might enable style changes, but the source does not establish repurchase rates or willingness to pay for this mechanism.
