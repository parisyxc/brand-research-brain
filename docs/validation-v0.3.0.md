No login or media playback occurred. Identity is based on the profile's self-identification/domain link, not verified control. The source records state that boundary; stale GUI-agent mirror content was not used as current strategy.

Product Hunt supplied first-party Maker introductions, a hunter credit, an activity-credit incentive and public questions/replies. These are launch/community evidence, not proof of paid creator marketing or conversion. Outside listings and introductions establish visible content/search-entry patterns, not deliberate brand outreach, keyword rank or SEO effectiveness. Instagram/TikTok/YouTube creator partnerships were not confirmed. No numeric keyword/traffic/paid campaign conclusions were invented.

[The reviewed example](../examples/growth/brain/wiki/growth/useit-growth.md) was saved in a blank Brain after replaying the original short case. No additional data-provider fees, API connections, registrations, logins, posts, creator messages or ads were used. Existing host/model/search quotas still apply.

## Limits

No creator sponsorship, channel conversion, keyword volume, reproducible rank, traffic or return on ad spend was established. AISA connectivity, account rights and prices were not tested. Obsidian UI, Claude integration and new-chat skill discovery are distinct from helper tests. Writes remain atomic per file rather than a multi-file database transaction.
