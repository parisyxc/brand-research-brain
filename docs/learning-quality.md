# Learning quality after the Doodle2Life comparison

## Diagnosis

Two outputs of one case differed in both evidence scope and editorial focus. The richer project output included additional competitor/community and gifting research; it was not a controlled comparison of instructions alone. The plugin Brain also had a later narrow growth investigation, whose access/identity limits spilled into the whole HTML cover and ideas.

The reusable gaps were real: abstract terms stood in for concrete buying situations; restrictions were more explicit than positive learning requirements; permissible hypothetical examples were not explained clearly enough; and growth refreshes relied on prose instructions alone to preserve the larger case.

## Changes in v0.5.1

- Read a shared learning-quality guide before intake, growth or presentation synthesis. Explain a person, situation, offer, alternative, tradeoff and small action when supported; this is an editorial lens, not more required pages.
- Allow explicitly hypothetical scenes and comparisons without treating them as verified audiences, demand or a selected niche. A bounded optional follow-up can answer a real category question; no automatic multi-channel market scan is introduced.
- Keep material uncertainty and source attribution while moving repeated shared qualifications and detailed account investigations out of the main reading narrative.
- Protect existing case fields on a growth-only HTML refresh. The `--growth-only` helper flag rejects changes to cover, facts, methods, category, ideas and case action, omitted prior basis, or absence of a saved growth note. Whole-case correction stays deliberate and available.
- Carry the learning purpose into both new-Brain guides. Existing personal preferences are preserved; the private project AGENTS.md is not bundled or copied into defaults.

## Acceptance and limits

v0.5.2 corrects a further category/application mix-up observed in CharCharms: the category card discussed the learner's proposed use before explaining water-bottle accessories and the case's distinctive role. Category explanation now proceeds from the studied category and product examples, to its evidenced product/category move, to a brief transferable takeaway. Candidate implementations remain in application/ideas. This is not a quota for innovations, a claim of historical first invention or proof of sales success.

Check actual output for a business a new reader can explain, source-grounded methods they could adapt, a meaningful comparison, a current-case hypothesis or honest evidence gap, and a concrete next step. “Participation matters” is not enough; a choice such as “family paints the blank object versus receiving a finished gift” makes the question usable. Apply the requirement to each case's evidence, not that example's category.

The Doodle2Life revision uses only the plugin Brain's original saved evidence. No richer project-only competitor, supplier or niche conclusions were imported. Existing source records and other cases remain intact. It is a primary-agent editorial repair and mechanical regression, not an independent fresh-agent behavior test or a new live research pass. Future new-chat use is still needed to assess consistency across brands. See [validation](validation.md).
